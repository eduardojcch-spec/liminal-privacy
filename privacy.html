LIMINAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

A dream journal holds intimate things. This page explains what we collect, where it goes and what you can do about it, without evasions.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Eduardo Julián Cortés Chamón, an independent developer based in Spain.

Contact: liminal.dreams.app@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and let you recover it.
  • The content of your dreams: what you write, the emotional state you select, and the interpretations the Oracle generates.
  • Your username and, if you choose to upload one, your profile picture.
  • Your community activity: posts, comments and reactions.
  • App settings: language, visual theme and notification preferences.
  • A notification identifier, if you agree to receive them, so we can send them.

We do not ask for or store your real name, your phone number, your location or your card details.

3. Your dreams are sent to Google to be analysed

This is the part that matters most, and you should know it before you write anything.

When you request an interpretation, the text of that dream is sent to Google's Gemini API, which is the artificial intelligence model that generates the analysis. Without that transfer there is no interpretation: it is the app's core function.

Google acts as a processor: it handles the text in order to return the analysis and, under its own terms for the paid API we use, it does not use that content to train its models.

The transfer happens only when you ask for an analysis. A dream you write and save without analysing never leaves our database.

If you would rather a particular dream did not go through that, save it without requesting an interpretation.

5. What we use your information for

To provide the service: storing your journal, generating analyses, showing your profile in the community if you take part, sending the reminders you have enabled, and managing your subscription.

The legal basis is performance of the contract you accept by using the app, except for notifications, which rest on your consent and which you can withdraw at any time from your profile or from your phone's settings.

6. What is private and what is not

Your journal is private. Nobody else can see your dreams or your analyses.

The only thing other people see is what you choose to publish in the community, together with your username and your profile picture if you have one.

7. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. When you delete it, your dreams, analyses, posts and profile are erased and cannot be recovered.

Payment records may be kept for as long as tax and commercial law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its processing, object to it, or receive it in a portable format. Write to liminal.dreams.app@gmail.com and we will reply.

The fastest way to exercise erasure is to delete your account from your profile settings: it is immediate and you do not need to ask.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority in your own country.

9. Minors

LIMINAL is for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. If we find an account belonging to someone under 16, we will delete it.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted and is stored on servers in the European Union with per-user access control: each account can only read its own. No system is infallible, but we do not store passwords in plain text or payment details.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change anything significant, we will update the date at the top and tell you inside the app.

12. Contact

For any privacy question: liminal.dreams.app@gmail.com.
